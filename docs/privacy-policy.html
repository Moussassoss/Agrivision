🌱 CropVana

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026

CropVana ("we", "our", or "us") is an AI-powered crop recommendation application designed to help smallholder farmers in Rwanda make informed planting decisions. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

We collect only the data necessary to provide crop recommendations. We do not sell, share, or rent your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you register, we collect:

  • Full name — to personalise your experience
  • Email address — for account login and password reset
  • Phone number (optional) — for future account recovery features
  • Password — stored as a bcrypt hash; we never store it in plain text

1.2 Location Data

With your explicit permission, we access your device's GPS coordinates (when the app is in use only). Your coordinates are used to:

  • Retrieve soil properties from the iSDAsoil satellite database
  • Retrieve local weather conditions from OpenWeather and NASA POWER
  • Generate a personalised crop recommendation

The latitude and longitude used for each recommendation are stored alongside that recommendation record in your history so you can review past results. We do not track your location continuously or in the background.

1.3 Usage Data (Recommendation History)

Each crop recommendation query stores:

  • GPS coordinates of the query
  • Soil properties (N, P, K, pH) derived from the iSDAsoil API for that location
  • Weather data (temperature, humidity, rainfall) for that location
  • The top crop recommendations returned by our ML model
  • Timestamp of the query

This data is linked to your user account and is visible only to you in the app's History screen.

1.4 Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect camera or photo library data
  • We do not collect contacts, calendar, or microphone data
  • We do not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID)
  • We do not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites

3. Third-Party Services

CropVana relies on the following external APIs to function. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • iSDAsoil — provides satellite-derived soil data at 30 m resolution. We send your GPS coordinates to their API. isda-africa.com
  • OpenWeather API — provides current and historical weather data. We send your GPS coordinates to their API. openweathermap.org/privacy-policy
  • NASA POWER API — provides long-term climatological rainfall data. We send your GPS coordinates to their API. This is a public US government API; no account is required.
  • Render — our backend hosting provider. Your data is stored on Render's PostgreSQL infrastructure. render.com/privacy

4. Data Retention

  • Account data: retained for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time by contacting us.
  • Recommendation history: retained for up to 2 years from the date of each query, then automatically deleted.
  • Passwords: stored as bcrypt hashes; plaintext passwords are never retained.

5. Data Security

  • All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS / TLS 1.2+
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (cost factor 12)
  • Authentication tokens (JWT) are stored in your device's Secure Store / Keychain and never in plain AsyncStorage
  • Our backend is hosted on Render with automatic SSL and private networking for the database connection

6. Children's Privacy

CropVana is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your recommendation history in JSON format
  • Withdraw consent for location access at any time via your device settings

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hamatguire@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of CropVana after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
