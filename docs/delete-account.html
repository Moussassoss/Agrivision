Delete your account

You can delete your CropVana account and all associated data at any time — directly from the app, or by contacting us.

⚠
This action is permanent. Deleting your account removes all your recommendations history, profile information, and cannot be undone.
Recommended

Delete from the app

1
Open CropVana
Make sure you're signed in to the account you want to delete.
2
Go to your Profile
Tap your initials avatar in the top-right corner of the home screen.
3
Scroll to Danger Zone
Scroll to the bottom of your profile page.
Danger Zone section
4
Tap "Delete my account" and confirm
Your account and all data will be permanently removed immediately.
Alternative

Request deletion by email

✉
Send a request to ssossinfo88@gmail.com
Include in your email: the email address registered on your CropVana account, and "Account Deletion Request" as the subject line. We'll process your request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Account name & email
Phone number
Crop recommendation history
Saved location data
Soil & weather queries
Authentication tokens
Anonymized, aggregated usage data that cannot identify you may be retained for improving the model. Password reset tokens are purged immediately on deletion.
